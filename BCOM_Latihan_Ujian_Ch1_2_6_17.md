## BAHAGIAN B — ESSAY (1 soalan, C4 Analysis)

> **Arahan:** Analyse the situation and give your opinion. Support your answer with concepts from Chapters 1, 2, 6 and 17. (Anggaran 20–25 markah)

**Scenario:**
Nadia is a project leader at a Malaysian software company. Her six-member team works partly from home. She posts important instructions in a group chat late at night and expects replies immediately. Two members, Aiman and Farah, disagree on the project direction; the rest stay quiet and agree with whatever the loudest member, Hakim, says. In the team meeting (20 people invited, no agenda), Hakim dominates, several people check their phones, and no minutes are kept. Nadia's final client report contains long, dense paragraphs, vague phrases, and a few spelling mistakes in the client's name. Her client presentation has 30 slides filled with small-font bullet points and she hands out copies of the slides at the start. The client says the proposal is unclear and the team's overall impression is unprofessional.

**Question:**
**Analyse the communication problems in this case and give your opinion on what Nadia should do to improve the team's communication, from teamwork and meetings through to report writing and presentation slides.**

### Rangka jawapan model (untuk rujukan semakan)

**Pendekatan C4:** pecahkan kes kepada masalah → kaitkan setiap satu dengan konsep → terangkan *kenapa* ia berlaku (sebab dan kesan) → beri pendapat/cadangan yang munasabah dan bernas.

| Masalah dalam kes | Analisis (konsep) | Pendapat / cadangan |
|---|---|---|
| Arahan lewat malam dan jangkaan balasan segera | Ch1 mobile: constant connectivity mengaburkan sempadan kerja/peribadi. Ch2 etiquette: respect boundaries of time | Tetapkan waktu komunikasi dan tanda "urgent" hanya bila benar-benar urgent |
| Ahli diam dan ikut Hakim | Ch2: **groupthink**; Hakim memegang peranan dysfunctional **controlling**; ketiadaan open and honest communication | Galakkan penyertaan, hargai pandangan berbeza, guna peranan encouraging/harmonizing |
| Aiman vs Farah | Ch2: konflik boleh membina atau memusnahkan; Nadia lambat bertindak (tiada **proactive behavior**) | Guna langkah resolusi: openness, research (fakta dahulu), flexibility, fair play, alliance |
| Mesyuarat tanpa agenda, 20 peserta, telefon, tiada minit | Ch2: lebih 10–12 orang sukar diurus; agenda wajib; penggunaan peranti tidak hormat; minit mesyuarat mencatat keputusan dan tanggungjawab | Jemput yang perlu sahaja, edar agenda, peraturan peranti, lantik pencatat minit |
| Laporan padat, kabur, silap ejaan | Ch6: kurang revision (content, organization, tone), readability, clarity (hedging, camouflaged verbs), conciseness, **proofreading** (semak nama klien, beberapa pass) | Buat revisi berperingkat, guna senarai/tajuk kecil, pendekkan perenggan (≤100 patah perkataan), proofread dengan teknik perceptual tricks |
| Slaid 30 muka penuh teks | Ch17: **death by PowerPoint**; slideument; melanggar 4–5 baris, satu idea per slaid; tiada key visual | Slaid ringkas, visual utama, perincian dalam handout |
| Handout diberi di awal | Ch17: audience boleh baca ke hadapan, terutama jika pendekatan tidak langsung | Beri handout selepas pembentangan atau beritahu apa yang akan diberi |
| Kesan: klien keliru, imej tidak profesional | Ch1: elements of professionalism; audiens menyamakan kualiti penulisan dengan kualiti pemikiran | Rumusan dan pendapat peribadi anda |

**Contoh perenggan pendapat (anda boleh adaptasi):**
> In my opinion, the root problem is not one individual mistake but the absence of a clear communication process. Nadia treats communication as sending messages rather than ensuring shared meaning, which Chapter 1 shows requires attention to the audience's expectations and context. Her team shows groupthink because no one feels safe to disagree, and this weakens the quality of decisions. The unmanaged meeting wastes time and cost, which are known disadvantages of teams. These weaknesses then flow into the final deliverables: the report lacks revision and proofreading, and the slides ignore the principle that visuals should support, not replace, the speaker. I believe Nadia should start by setting team norms and a meeting structure, because every later improvement (clear reports, better slides) depends on a team that communicates openly and respects each other's time.

**Petua markah:**
- Jangan hanya senaraikan teori (itu C1/C2). Setiap konsep mesti dihubungkan dengan **bukti dalam kes**.
- Tunjukkan **sebab-akibat** (contoh: groupthink → keputusan lemah → laporan kabur).
- Beri **pendapat jelas** ("In my opinion / I believe …") dengan justifikasi, dan nyatakan **keutamaan** tindakan.
- Struktur: pengenalan ringkas → analisis ikut tema → cadangan/pendapat → kesimpulan.
